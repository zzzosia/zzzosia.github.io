He is The First one. A true king, a black knight in a body of a little chonky cocker spaniel.

Named after Bob Marley and I think they're quite alike. He taught me a lot and I taught him... well, at least something, I think. He has a lot of his own opinion on everything, a very strong and rude yet so funny character. He is the biggest sloth I know, sleeping like, 22 hours a day. The best hugger in the world.

When he was younger, he was my little agility master, a champion of our own backyard. While I am working or studying, he is a huge couch potato, but when we were on a hike... the most courageous creature I know! The best hiker, adventurer, he has a great desire to go further and further, sniff and piss on everything he can. His biggest passion? Eating. Really. He gets fat from even looking at food, keeping him fit is our greatest challange of all times. He comes from a very good english cocker spaniel show-line but he wasn't even to a dog show once - the black sheep of his family, literally and figuratively ;)

When I am asked to think of a best day of my life, I recall the day we got Him. Marley is my little patronus. He's such a funny fluffy little creature with a mind of an old, wise man.
